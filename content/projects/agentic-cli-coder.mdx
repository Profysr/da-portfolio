## The Vision

Refactoring is repetitive. An agent that:
1. Reads your codebase (Tree-sitter AST)
2. Proposes changes (local LLM via Ollama)
3. Shows diffs in terminal (Rich)
4. Applies only after your approval

## Current Capabilities

- `agentic refactor --pattern "extract_method" --target src/utils.py`
- `agentic rename --from old_name --to new_name --scope project`
- `agentic add_types --file src/models.py`

## Why Local LLMs?

- Zero latency for small diffs
- No code leaves your machine
- Free (run on your GPU)

## The Metric Overload Problem

Dashboards cluttered with 50+ vanity KPIs create noise rather than clarity. Effective product and data teams narrow focus to leading indicators that genuinely reflect user value.

## High-Leverage Metrics

### 1. Feature Depth vs. Surface Adoption
Measure repeat user interaction within 7 days of initial adoption instead of raw page visits.

### 2. Time-to-First-Value (TTFV)
Track elapsed duration from account provisioning to the completion of the core workspace action.
